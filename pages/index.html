Verified workflows: sample code

From the paper Verification Is All You Need (Part 2 of the Agent Middleground Protocol papers). Today’s processes, reimagined as workflows shared across people and organisations, where the authorised people are the humans in the loop who verify and approve their own parts.

the main way

Buying a car with insurance and a loan

The motor department runs the workflow on its own server. The showroom, the insurer, the bank and the buyer each approve their own part, in their roles, with their own swipe. The data is shown: every party of the case sees the whole case file.

A sealed tender to build a bridge

The tender runs in an Agent Middleground. Companies submit sealed bids, the published rules score them, and two of three officers approve the result. The data stays hidden: each company takes back only its own score.

Try the sealed tender

Everything runs on this computer, and every name is made up. A ticked box stands in for the fingerprint unless you link this device’s fingerprint (a passkey) to a person. Then only that fingerprint approves for them.
