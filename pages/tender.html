Another possibility: a workflow in a middle ground. The City Works Office invites bids. Each company’s authorised signatory submits a sealed bid with their own swipe. At the deadline the published rules check eligibility and score the bids. The evaluation committee sees only the scores, and two of its three officers approve the result. Each company takes back a confirmation token with its own score; nobody else’s price or accounts ever leave.
